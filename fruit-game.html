🍉 Fruit Run

You are a tiny blueberry on a very long platform. Race to the end — and get there as the biggest fruit you can.

Match to grow. Grab fruits that are the same as you. Match enough and you ripen into the next fruit up.
Mind the edges. Roll off the platform and the run is over. Watch for narrow bits and holes.
Dodge the kitchen. 🔪 knives, 🪓 cleavers and 🌀 blenders knock you down a size. 🥖 rollers and 🧊 ice can be jumped.
Pick your gate. Glowing gates come in pairs — one may ripen you, the other may slice you. Choose a side.
Controls. ← → or A / D to steer, Space to jump, P to pause. On a phone: the buttons at the bottom, or drag anywhere and tap to jump.
